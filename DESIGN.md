# Design direction

The site uses a developer field-notes direction rather than a generic AI-product aesthetic.

- Dark, graphite-ground palette with one vermilion action colour.
- Condensed display type, humanist body type, and a mono data layer for navigation, tags, and the visit count.
- Personal photographs carry the hero. Project imagery is real project material or a simple technical diagram.
- The photo stack is a functional, user-controlled gallery. It uses arrows, keyboard controls, and touch swipes.
- Surfaces are flat and defined with borders. There are no glow effects, decorative gradients, glass cards, floating orbs, custom cursors, or scroll-in animations.
- Project cards gain emphasis from imagery, a fine border, and an underlined link rather than shadows or motion.

Keep new content concise and factual. Use sentence case for navigation and buttons, do not use em dashes, and avoid feature-card grids with generic icons.
